图 1｜Fig. 1 | 鲁棒程序化模型拟合框架。程序化模型经生成与采样后，通过鲁棒相似度与不完美观测进行比较；CCO、CS、PSO 和 DE 通过统一接口更新候选参数，并在相同参数边界、配对随机种子和函数评估预算下进行公平比较。 Robust procedural model fitting framework. A procedural model is generated and sampled, then compared with imperfect observations using a robust similarity measure. CCO, CS, PSO and DE update candidate parameters through a shared interface and are compared under identical bounds, paired seeds and function-evaluation budgets.
